# Image Prompt Library

Reusable prompts. The first one is the exact text that produced
`assets/generated/hero-01.png` — it is proven, so start from it rather than rewriting.

## The style block (paste into every prompt)

```
Vivid spring-green body (#43C455), sunny golden-yellow roof (#FFF091),
tangerine-coral bumpers and window trim (#FF854F), violet-purple wheels (#5A2A8F)
with coral hubcaps, round cream headlights. Huge round eyes filling the windshield
with big white sclera, large dark glossy pupils and two bright sparkle catchlights
each, rosy blush cheeks, wide open happy smile below the windshield. Chunky rounded
toy proportions, glossy smooth plastic surfaces. Candy-bright saturated high-contrast
colours, NOT pastel. Soft bright lighting, cheerful preschool cartoon look,
glossy smooth 3D animation render. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos,
no watermark.
```

**Always attach `assets/generated/turnaround-01.png` as a reference image.** That is
what keeps the character consistent between sessions.

## 1. Hero shot (PROVEN — this made hero-01.png)

Model used: Nano Banana 2, image-to-image, 16:9, 1K, with the original bus as reference.

```
Restyle this cute 3D cartoon toy bus into a bright, eye-catching hero shot for a
preschool nursery-rhyme cartoon aimed at toddlers.

KEEP THE SAME CHARACTER IDENTITY: the same chunky rounded toy-bus body shape, the
same rounded roof, and the same color scheme — mint-green body, cream-yellow roof,
coral-orange bumpers and window trim, lavender-purple wheels with coral hubcaps, and
round headlights.

CHANGE THESE THINGS:
1. COLOR: push the saturation much higher. Vivid spring-green body, sunny
   golden-yellow roof, punchy tangerine-coral trim, rich violet wheels. Candy-bright,
   high-contrast, glossy colors — NOT pastel, NOT washed out, NOT muted.
2. FACE: make the eyes dramatically bigger — two huge round eyes filling the
   windshield, with large white sclera, big dark glossy pupils, and bright white
   sparkle catchlights, looking directly at the camera. Add soft rosy blush cheeks and
   a wide, open, happy smile with a warm curved mouth on the front of the bus below
   the windshield.
3. SHOT: one single bus, front three-quarter view, low camera angle looking slightly
   upward so the bus feels big, friendly and heroic, caught mid-bounce as it drives
   toward the camera.
4. BACKGROUND: a simple bright cartoon city street — clean rounded colorful buildings,
   vivid blue sky, big fluffy white clouds, warm sunshine, a few simple green trees.
   Keep the background simple and uncluttered so the bus clearly pops in front of it.
5. STYLE: glossy smooth 3D animation render, soft rounded plastic toy surfaces, soft
   bright lighting, thick clean shapes, high contrast, cheerful preschool cartoon look.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 2. Expression sheet

```
Create a clean expression sheet of this exact cartoon bus character on a plain flat
white background. Show the front of the bus four times in a 2x2 grid, identical in
every way except the face:
- top left: HAPPY — eyes wide open, big open smile
- top right: SURPRISED — eyes very wide, small round O mouth, eyebrows raised
- bottom left: SLEEPY — eyelids half closed, small contented smile
- bottom right: EXCITED — eyes squeezed into happy upward arcs, huge open grin
[STYLE BLOCK]
Even flat lighting, no cast shadows, no scenery, same scale in every view.
```

## 3. Scene backgrounds (generate without the bus, composite later)

Cheaper and more reusable than regenerating the bus in every location.

```
A simple bright 3D cartoon [LOCATION] for a preschool children's animation, empty
with no characters and no vehicles. Clean rounded shapes, candy-bright saturated
colours, vivid blue sky with big fluffy white clouds, warm sunshine, uncluttered
composition with clear empty space in the centre foreground where a character will
be placed. Glossy smooth 3D animation render style. No text, no letters, no
watermark. 16:9.
```

Swap `[LOCATION]` for: `city street with colourful shops`, `school with a playground`,
`park with big green trees and a pond`, `beach with gentle waves`, `farm with a red
barn`, `zoo entrance`, `night-time street with warm glowing streetlamps`,
`rainy street with puddles`.

## 4. Counting / lineup shot

```
[N] cartoon buses in the same glossy 3D toy-cartoon style, lined up in a neat row
facing the camera on a bright cartoon street, arranged from largest at the left to
smallest at the right. The green hero bus is at the front of the line. Each bus is a
different cheerful saturated colour but shares the same chunky rounded proportions and
the same face design. All of them smile directly at the viewer.
[STYLE BLOCK]
```

## 5. Thumbnail plate

```
Extreme close-up hero shot of the green cartoon bus character, its face filling most
of the frame, looking directly at the camera with a huge happy open smile, low camera
angle. Bright blue sky with fluffy white clouds behind it. Leave clear empty sky space
in the top right corner of the image for a text badge to be added later.
[STYLE BLOCK]
16:9.
```

The empty corner is deliberate — that is where the "40 min" badge goes.
