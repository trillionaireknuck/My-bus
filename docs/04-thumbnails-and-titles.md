# Packaging — Thumbnails and Titles

Packaging is sold to the **parent**, not the toddler. The child never sees the
thumbnail; the parent scrolling a phone at 7am does.

## Thumbnail rules

Reverse-engineered from the two videos in your reference screenshot:

1. **A face, large, eyes to camera.** Ella's face should fill 40–60% of the frame.
   Crop in tighter than feels comfortable — thumbnails are viewed at ~120px wide.
2. **Runtime badge, top right.** "40 min", "1 HOUR". This is the single highest-value
   element for a parent. Both reference thumbnails had one.
3. **Three or more characters** when the video is a compilation — variety promises
   that the child will not get bored.
4. **Saturated colours on a bright sky.** Blue sky background makes the bus pop.
5. **Little or no text besides the runtime.** Toddler-content thumbnails that work are
   almost wordless. The title carries the words.
6. **Low camera angle.** Looking slightly up at the bus makes it feel big and friendly.

`assets/generated/hero-01.png` is built to these rules already — crop it to 1280×720,
add a runtime badge, and it is a working thumbnail.

Keep a consistent thumbnail *style* across uploads. Parents should recognise your
channel in a grid without reading the name.

## Title formula

Traditional-melody titles carry the search volume. Structure:

```
[SONG NAME] | [RUNTIME] of [CATEGORY] | [CHARACTER / CHANNEL]
```

Real examples in that shape:

- `Wheels On The Bus | 40 Minutes of Nursery Rhymes | Ella the Bus`
- `Different Types Of Buses! | Learn Vehicles for Toddlers | 30 Min`
- `10 Little Buses | Counting Songs for Kids | 1 Hour`
- `Bus Colour Song | Learn Colours for Babies | Ella the Bus`

**Why this order:** the searched term goes first, because titles truncate. Everything
after the first ~50 characters may not display on mobile.

## Words that pull in this niche

Front-load these where they are honest: `Wheels on the Bus`, `Nursery Rhymes`,
`Kids Songs`, `Toddlers`, `Babies`, `Learn Colours` / `Colors`, `Counting`,
`Bedtime`, `Sleep`, `1 Hour`, `40 Minutes`, `Compilation`, `Vehicles for Kids`.

Use both `Colours` and `Colors` across your catalogue — the spellings serve different
markets.

## What not to do

- **No clickbait.** This audience is parents choosing what a 2-year-old watches. A
  misleading thumbnail loses the channel, not just the view.
- **No scary or startling imagery**, ever, including in thumbnails.
- **Do not put your logo in the thumbnail** at the expense of the face.
- **Do not imitate another channel's thumbnail layout closely enough to confuse a
  parent.** Learn the grammar, not the specific design.
