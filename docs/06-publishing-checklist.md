# Publishing Checklist

## Before you upload anything: "Made for Kids"

This content is directed at children under 13, so under COPPA you must mark it
**"Made for Kids"** on YouTube. This is not optional and the penalties for getting it
wrong are real.

**Know what that setting costs you**, because it changes the whole business model:

| Disabled on Made-for-Kids videos | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Personalised ads | Substantially lower ad rates than general content |
| Comments | No community building in comments — at all |
| Notifications to subscribers | New uploads do not notify. Subscribers matter less. |
| Save to playlist, Stories, live chat | Fewer discovery surfaces |
| End screens and cards | You cannot link the next video with an end screen |

**What this means strategically:** you cannot grow this channel through community.
You grow it through *packaging* (thumbnail + title), *catalogue depth*, and *watch
time*. That is why the earlier docs put so much weight on those three things.

## Per-upload checklist

- [ ] Marked **Made for Kids**
- [ ] Thumbnail is 1280×720, face large, runtime badge, eyes to camera
- [ ] Title follows the formula, searched term first
- [ ] Description: 2–3 sentences, then the full chapter list
- [ ] **Chapters added** — first timestamp must be `00:00`, minimum three chapters,
      each at least 10 seconds long, or YouTube will not render them
- [ ] Tags: song name, "nursery rhymes", "kids songs", "toddlers", character name
- [ ] Added to a themed playlist (playlists still work and still drive sessions)
- [ ] Audio checked on a phone speaker, not just headphones
- [ ] Watched the first 10 seconds back — is Benny's face on screen by 0:03?
- [ ] No flashing, strobing, or sudden loud sounds anywhere

## Chapter format for the description

```
00:00 Wheels on the Bus
02:14 Benny's Colour Route
04:38 Count the Buses
07:01 Old MacDonald
```

## Upload cadence

Consistency beats volume. One good song a week, published the same day each week,
beats five in a burst and then nothing for a month. The algorithm and the parents
both reward a channel that reliably has something new.

## What to watch in analytics

Ignore subscriber count — it is a weak signal on Made-for-Kids channels because
notifications are off. Watch instead:

- **Average view duration** — the number that matters. If it collapses in the first
  15 seconds, your opening is wrong (almost always a logo sting or a slow start).
- **Impressions click-through rate** — if this is low, the thumbnail is the problem,
  not the video.
- **Traffic sources** — "Browse features" and "Suggested video" growing over time is
  the sign the algorithm has understood your channel.

## Safety and trust

- No scary imagery, no jump scares, no distress, no peril, ever.
- No real-world unsafe behaviour presented as fun.
- Do not imitate another channel's characters, names, or thumbnail designs closely
  enough to confuse a parent. Beyond the legal risk, YouTube actively demotes
  "low-quality kids content" that mimics established brands.
