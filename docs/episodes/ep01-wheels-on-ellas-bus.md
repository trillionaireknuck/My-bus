> The wheels on Ella go round and round,

bedtime, which is one of the highest-volume searches in this category.

## Recording direction

- **Tempo:** 112 BPM for verses 1–5, drop to ~70 for verse 6.
- **Voice:** female or child-range sits best for this age group. Warm, unhurried,
  slightly over-articulated. Smile while you sing — it is audible.
- **Record the chorus once and reuse the take** for all six repeats. Saves hours and
  makes the song tighter.
- **Backing:** ukulele, glockenspiel, light percussion. Keep the low end thin — most
  listening happens on phone and tablet speakers.
- **No sudden volume jumps or startling effects**, anywhere.
- Record in the quietest room you have, soft furnishings around you, phone or mic
  about a hand's width away and slightly off-axis to avoid plosives.

Free tools: **Audacity** to record, **MuseScore** to lay out the melody,
**LMMS** (or GarageBand on Mac) for the backing.

## Assembling the final cut

1. Drop `assets/video/ep01-picture-lock.mp4` on the timeline in DaVinci Resolve.
2. Drop your audio underneath and line the first downbeat to **0:08.6**.
3. If your tempo differs, slide the shot boundaries — every shot loops seamlessly, so
   you can trim or extend any of them without a visible seam.
4. Export 1080p H.264.
5. Upload, **mark Made for Kids**, add chapters (`docs/06-publishing-checklist.md`).
